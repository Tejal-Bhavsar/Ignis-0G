01 · Problem Statement & Non-Intuitive Physics Slide 2 / 7
Why Microgravity Fire ≠ Earth Fire

Fragmented PSI Data Hides Critical Exploration Fire Risks

Buoyancy Absence
BASS-II Goldilocks Flow (5–15 cm/s)
Without buoyancy, flames form diffusion spheres. Low spacecraft ventilation (peak ~10 cm/s) creates a Goldilocks hazard where solid flames spread upstream at lower O₂% than Earth tests.
Exploration Atmospheres
34% O₂ at 8.2 psia (Lunar SEA)
To reduce EVA pre-breathe time from 4.3 psia 100% O₂ suits, Lunar habitats & rovers raise O₂% and cut N₂ thermal ballast—dramatically accelerating fabric, plastic, and wire flammability.
Empirical Blind Spots
0.2 µm Smoke & Partial-g Gaps
SAME proved microgravity fires emit 0.2 µm aerosols below ISS 1.0 µm alarms. Meanwhile, PSI has 0 direct 1/6g or 3/8g solid-flammability studies (pending LUCI & FM2).
